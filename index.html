Nobody is short of people. They're short of a reason to show up.

A twenty-seven year old in Sector 56 lives within three kilometres of dozens of people who play badminton on Sunday morning. He will never meet one of them. There is no list, no invitation, and no acceptable way to knock.

Dating apps solve a different problem and carry the wrong intent. Group chats fill up with plans nobody schedules. What is missing is not connection. It is a specific court, at a specific hour, with two spots open.

BOND only does that last part. Every plan on it has a place, a time, a price and a number of seats.

How it works

  1. Tell it what you're into

    Pick three or more from badminton, gym, trekking, café hopping, live music, gaming. That calibrates what you get shown.

  2. See what's on near you

    Your feed fills with plans sorted by distance and overlap. Every card shows how far, how much, and how many seats are left.

  3. Book and turn up

    Pay in the app, get a QR pass, scan it at the venue. No group chat, no coordination, no one flaking in the thread.

Two kinds of plans

Verified host

Curated circles

Run by venues and hosts we've checked. Fixed price, fixed capacity, instant pass. A district badminton tournament, a sunrise yoga session, a rooftop table on Friday.

Community run

Peer initiatives

Anything a member wants to organise. Court fees split four ways, a coffee and sketch walk, a Sunday trek. Host it free, set a price or split the cost.
