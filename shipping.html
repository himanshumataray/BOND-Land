Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

In short: BOND sells digital passes for local plans. Nothing is shipped. Your pass is delivered instantly in the BOND app and by email once payment succeeds.

What you receive

When you book a plan on BOND you receive a digital pass with a QR code for that plan. BOND does not sell or ship physical goods.

Delivery time

Your pass is issued immediately after your payment is confirmed by our payment partner, Razorpay. It appears in the BOND app under your bookings and is sent to the email address on your account. In rare cases of a payment delay, it may take up to 30 minutes.

Using your pass

Show the QR code at the venue at the time of the plan. Add-ons such as gear rental are provided at the venue by the host.

Didn't receive your pass?

If your payment went through but you don't see a pass within 30 minutes, email sudarshanmanojarora@gmail.com with your payment reference. We will issue the pass or refund the payment in full, as described in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
